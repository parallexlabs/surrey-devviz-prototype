Civic investments and destinations

  • City Centre Arena (planned) (Civic investment). On September 10, 2026, the City of Surrey reported that demolition had begun at the site of the planned 10,000-seat City Centre Arena at 10355 King George Boulevard. The City gives 2030 as the planned opening. Planned venue; not open to visitors. Read the City's update
  • Surrey City Hall and Civic Plaza (Civic place). City Hall and Civic Plaza, at 13450 104 Avenue, are among the civic places the City lists for City Centre. City Centre on surrey.ca
  • City Centre Library (Civic place). City Centre Library, at 10350 University Drive, is one of the civic and cultural places the City lists for City Centre. City Centre on surrey.ca
  • SFU Surrey (Campus). Simon Fraser University's Surrey campus is one of the institutions the City lists for City Centre. City Centre on surrey.ca
  • Holland Park (Destination). Holland Park is one of the parks the City lists for City Centre. City Centre on surrey.ca
